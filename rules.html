Official 10U Rules

Key playing standards for the 2027 Nickel City Baseball League. The full official rulebook is available below.

Download Official Rulebook

Game Format

  • 70-foot bases / 46-foot pitching mound.
  • Six innings; no new inning begins after 2:00 hours.
  • A game is complete after four complete innings.
  • Minimum of eight players to play.

Batting & Defense

  • Entire bench bats.
  • Defensive substitutions may be made freely.
  • Dropped third strike and infield fly rules apply.
  • No metal spikes.

Bats & Base Running

  • USA Bat rules are enforced.
  • Lead-offs and stealing are allowed, including home.
  • No head-first slides into a base; diving back is permitted.
  • Runners must slide to avoid a collision when a fielder is positioned to make a play.

Pitching

  • Maximum nine consecutive outs per game.
  • Once removed from the mound, a player may not pitch again that game.
  • Three hit batters in one inning requires removal from pitching.
  • Second mound visit in the same inning requires removal.
  • One balk warning per pitcher.

Run & Mercy Rules

  • Maximum six runs per inning, except the sixth inning.
  • Sixth inning allows unlimited runs.
  • During playoffs, the six-run inning limit does not apply.
  • Twelve-run mercy rule begins after 3½ innings when the home team is ahead.

Sportsmanship

  • Unsportsmanlike behavior by players, coaches, parents or fans will not be tolerated.
  • Managers are responsible for team and fan behavior.
  • Ejected coaches/managers receive a one-game suspension under the rulebook.
